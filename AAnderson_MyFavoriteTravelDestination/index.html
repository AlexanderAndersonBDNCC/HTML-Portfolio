My Favorite Travel Destination: SPACE

If you could go anywhere, why not go to space!

(If you have been here before you can jump to the Packing List)

Why Space?

Space is, arguably, one of the closest travel destinations to a majority of people on Earth. It is only about 62 miles away (100km) at any given time. It is also the absolute largest travel destination in existence!
If you would like a reference to the ridiculous size of space, I recommend Neal.fun's Size of Space interactive website!

My Favorite Aspects

  • All the different stars you can see
  • Microgravity
  • All the different planets! My Favorites are:
    1. Saturn
    2. Jupiter
    3. Mars
  • Galaxies
  • And finally, Space Stations!

Items You Should Pack

  • Your favorite sleeping bag
    • You will need to attach this to a wall to catch some zzz's!
  • A Capillary (Zero-G) Cup
  • Several months of food
  • A few jumpsuits to wear
  • A rag to wash yourself with
  • A radio to phone-home
